This key

Free move: any dot, knees and elbows too, goes exactly where you drag it in that view, and the body stretches to follow. Then put the bones back to their own lengths: "hands & feet stay" keeps them where you put them and bends elbows and knees toward where you put those; "angles stay" keeps every bone pointing where you aimed it and lets the hand or foot at the end land where it lands. Changing key or saving does "hands & feet stay" for you.
