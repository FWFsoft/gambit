Centre joints stay on the centre line. The right side always mirrors the left. Dashed lines end at a bone tip (top of head, fingertips, toes), not a joint.

What right looks like

  • Every dot sits inside the body, at the real joint.
  • Shoulders inside the deltoids, not on the neck or on top of the traps.
  • Hips at the top of the thigh; knees and elbows where the limb bends.
  • Check the side view too: a joint can look right from the front and sit in front of the body.
